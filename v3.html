Open strings

E, A, D, G from thickest to thinnest. Each string is a fourth above the one before it.

Two short steps

E to F and B to C sit one fret apart. Every other pair of neighbouring natural notes is two frets apart.

Fret 12

The same letter as the open string, one octave higher. The notes then repeat.

Fret 5 and the octave shape

Fret 5 matches the next open string (E gives A, A gives D, D gives G). For an octave, skip a string and move up two frets.
